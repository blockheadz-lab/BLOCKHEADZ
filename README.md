# BLOCKHEADZ

4444 generative NFTs on Ethereum.

[Twitter](https://x.com/BLOCKHEADZETH) · [Website](#)

---

4444 unique voxel characters generated with p5.js WebGL. Every trait is pulled from real trait pools — no post-generation filtering, no manual curation. What comes out is what gets minted.

There's a rare mask system buried in the collection. You won't know until you mint.
